Privacy Policy

Who We Are

This privacy policy covers Walkie n Talkie, which is operated by Hemant Singh, together with the related website or services described here. It covers the app on iOS, iPadOS, and macOS.

What Data We Collect

Information users provide directly may include:

  • Account and profile details such as Email address, Name, and Account identifier
  • Contact information people provide through the app or related services
  • Contact data used by contact-based features

Information collected automatically through the app or related services may include:

  • Crash logs, diagnostics, and reliability data
  • Identifiers needed to recognize an account, device, session, or subscription state

Account access may use sign-in methods such as Email and password and Sign in with Apple.

Some data leaves the device to support app functionality such as accounts, syncing, purchases, support, or other online features.

Some of this data may be linked to a person, account, or persistent identifier.

Information from Other Sources

We may also receive information from Social login providers when people choose to use those sources with the app or related services.

How We Use Data

We use your data in a variety of ways, including:

  • To understand product usage and improve the app.
  • To find bugs and keep the app stable.
  • To support advertising-related features included in the app or related services.
  • To support tracking-related behavior where that is needed for the features and services described in this policy.
  • To personalize the app experience for the user.
  • To manage billing-related access and entitlements.
  • To send Reminders.

The following providers are used:

  • Advertising: AdMob. Used for showing Ads.
  • Authentication: Firebase Auth. Used for sign In and account management.
  • Backend: Firebase. Used for channel management.
  • CDN / Infrastructure: CloudFlare. Used for for TURN servers to assist WebRTC.
  • Crash Reporting / Performance: Crashlytics. Used for crash diagnostics.

Additional feature details:

  • Contacts: Used to invite friends.
  • Microphone: Used for the walkie talkie feature.
  • Push Notifications: Used to send notifications when a friens joins a channel to connect and the other party is not yet joined.
  • StoreKit: For Pro Subscriptions.

When Data is Shared

When an app feature needs online processing, syncing, account handling, or support, relevant data may be sent off the device to operate that feature.

Some data is shared with outside service providers that help operate the app or related services.

The following providers are used:

  • Advertising: AdMob. Used for showing Ads.
  • Authentication: Firebase Auth. Used for sign In and account management. May receive personal data needed for this role.
  • Backend: Firebase. Used for channel management. May receive personal data needed for this role.
  • CDN / Infrastructure: CloudFlare. Used for for TURN servers to assist WebRTC.
  • Crash Reporting / Performance: Crashlytics. Used for crash diagnostics.

Payments and Subscriptions

The app offers in-app purchases or subscriptions through StoreKit.

Social Logins and Public Features

The app may let people register or sign in using Sign in with Apple. Authentication providers include Firebase Auth. When someone signs in this way, we may receive account or profile details such as Email address, Name, and Account identifier.

The app or related services include Public profiles. Information people post or share through these features may be visible to other users or to the public, depending on how the feature works.

Data Retention

We keep data until the user deletes it, closes the account, or asks us to remove it, unless a longer retention period is legally required.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data, but no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
